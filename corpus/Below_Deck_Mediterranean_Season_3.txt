The '''third season''' of [[Below Deck Mediterranean]] was aired on Bravo from May 15, 2018, for seventeen weeks and concluded on September 11, 2018. It aired a total of seventeen episodes.

==About==
For this season three cast members returned from the previous season, this included Captain [[Sandy Yawn]], Chef [[Adam Glick]] and Chief Stewardess [[Hannah Ferrier]]. 

Captain Sandy was in charge of commanding the yacht ''Talisman Maiton'' around the Italian coast during the charter season. ''Talisman Maiton'' was a 177-foot long superyacht with a sundeck spa pool, a full gym, and an observation deck with panoramic views. It accommodates up to 12 guests across six individually styled cabins, including a master suite, one VIP cabin, and four double/twin convertible cabins.

== Notable Storylines ==

*'''Hannah and Conrad's rocky romance:''' Their relationship is a major focus, with Captain Sandy expressing concern about their growing feelings impacting their work performance, and Hannah facing questions about her commitment to the relationship.
*'''Kasey's questionable past:''' The crew becomes suspicious of Kasey's credibility after discovering potential inconsistencies in her resume, leading to tension within the interior team.
*'''Chef Adam's struggles:''' Adam faces challenges with demanding guests and inconsistent quality in the kitchen, sometimes leading to conflict with the crew.
*'''Crew hierarchy issues:''' Hannah confronts both Kasey and another crew member, Brooke, regarding their understanding of the interior hierarchy and responsibilities.
*'''Captain Sandy's leadership:''' Captain Sandy is often seen navigating the interpersonal drama on the boat, particularly in relation to Hannah and Conrad's relationship.
