The '''fourth season''' of ''[[Original Below Deck list|Below Deck]]'' was aired on Bravo from September 6, 2016, for fourteen weeks and concluded on December 6, 2016.

== About ==
For this seasons cast three crew returned from the previous season; captain [[Lee Rosbach]], chef [[Ben Robinson]] and chief stewardess [[Kate Chastain]]. It also saw the return of [[Below Deck Season 2|season two's]] [[Kelley Johnson]] as the boat's newly promoted bosun, replacing [[Eddie Lucas]] who decided not to return. 

The yacht featured in this season was a 154-foot superyacht called ''Valor'', which had an  Aft deck, sky lounge, and a wide range of water toys. For the interior it had a Hampton beach house-style with a monochrome palette, and contemporary décor; it featured six en-suite cabins, including a master suite and VIP stateroom; accommodating 12 guests and 11 crew members. The season was filmed in the US Virgin Islands.

==Notable Storylines==

*'''New Crew Members:'''  The season introduces new deckhands, including Trevor who creates conflict with his attitude and work ethic.
*'''Kate and Emily's Dynamic:'''  Kate struggles to manage Emily's lack of attention to detail as a junior stewardess.
*'''Ben and Emily's Romance:'''  Chef Ben and Emily start a relationship which causes friction within the crew.
*'''Kelley's Leadership Issues:'''  Captain Lee confronts Kelley about his decisions as Bosun, including an incident where a crew member is left stranded on a beach
*'''Kyle's Love Life:''' Deckhand Kyle went from going on a date with Sierra when he first arrived to proposing to his girlfriend from back home by the end of it
