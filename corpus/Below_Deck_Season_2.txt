The '''second season''' of ''[[Original Below Deck list|Below Deck]]'' was aired on Bravo from August 12, 2014, for thirteen weeks and concluded on November 4, 2014. 

== About ==
This seasons cast saw [[Lee Rosbach]] return as captain, [[Ben Robinson]] as chef and [[Kat Held]] as the 2nd stewardess. [[Eddie Lucas]] also returned but as the new bosun; a promotion from being the 2nd deckhand last season. This season also featured the first siblings onboard a boat for the franchise ([[Kelley Johnson|Kelley]] and [[Amy Johnson]]), as well as the first of six seasons for chief stewardess [[Kate Chastain]].

Captain Lee was in charge this season of commanding the yacht ''Ohana'', around the Caribbean Sea and the Atlantic Ocean, docking in the lovely British Virgin Islands. ''Ohana'' is a 154-foot superyacht with a large sundeck, outdoor bar, and has accommodations for 10 guests.

== Notable Storylines ==

* '''Central Conflict:''' Kate's strict leadership style creates tension with the other crew members, especially the interior team
* '''Kelley's and Jennice Relationship:''' The two deckhand's start to have romantic feelings for each other, but Jennice has a boyfriend back home
* '''Andrew's Work Ability:''' From day one new deckhand Andrew's yachting experience and abilities were questioned 
* '''Kat and Amy's Friendship:''' The fallout of the stewardesses previous friendship comes to alight after the return of last seasons chief stewardess Adrienne.
