The '''fifth season''' of [[Below Deck Mediterranean]] was aired on Bravo from June 1, 2020, for twenty-one weeks and concluded on October 26, 2020. It aired a total of twenty-two episodes.
==About==
At the beginning of the season we saw Captain [[Sandy Yawn]], Chief Stewardess [[Hannah Ferrier]] and from episode thirteen [[Aesha Scott]] return from the previous season. Also returning was season two's [[Malia White]] as the new Bosun and [[Christine "Bugsy" Drake]] as the 2nd Stewardess (later Chief Stewardess).

Captain Sandy was in charge of commanding the yacht ''The Wellington'' around the Spanish island Mallorca during this charter season. ''The Wellington'' is a 184.5-foot superyacht, with a 1920's inspired décor in a black and cream colour palette. It features a beach club, spa, and multiple outdoor lounging areas. The yacht accommodates up to 12 guests in five staterooms.

==Notable Storylines==

* '''Hannah's departure:''' The central drama of the season is Hannah Ferrier, the Chief Stew, being fired by Captain Sandy after Malia reports finding prescription medication on board, causing significant tension within the interior team.
*'''Jess and Rob's relationship:''' Jess and Rob, crew members on the yacht, have a whirlwind romance marked by arguments and passionate moments, adding a significant relationship dynamic to the season.
* '''Second Stew chaos:''' Following Lara's departure, the interior team struggles with a new second stew, creating further challenges for Hannah and later, the new Chief Stew.
*'''Kiko's struggles:''' Kiko, a chef, faces significant criticism from Captain Sandy for his performance, leading to a tense dynamic and eventual departure from the series.
*'''Malia's leadership:''' As the Bosun, Malia is often involved in resolving conflicts and managing the deck crew, sometimes clashing with other crew members.
*'''Guest drama:''' The season also features various challenging charter guests who create difficulties for the crew, adding to the overall tension
