The '''first season''' of [[Below Deck Mediterranean]] was aired on Bravo from May 3, 2016, for thirteen weeks and concluded on July 26, 2016. It aired a total of thirteen episodes.

==About==
In season one we were introduced to a new crew on a luxury yacht navigating the Greek islands, led by Captain [[Mark Howard]], as they face the challenges of serving demanding charter guests while navigating their own personal relationships and professional hurdles, including a new chief stew, [[Hannah Ferrier|Hannah]], and Chef [[Ben Robinson]], all while working on the "Ionian Princess" during a charter season in the Mediterranean Sea.

It was filmed in the Mediterranean Sea around the Greek islands, on board a 150-foot superyacht called ''Ionian Princess''. It featured a large sundeck with jacuzzi, elegant interior design, six cabins for upto 12 guests including a full-beam master suite and water toys.

==Notable Storylines==

*'''Danny's problematic behaviour:''' Danny frequently disrespects the chain of command, disobeys Captain Mark, and engages in flirtatious behavior with guests, often causing tension with the other crew members.
*'''Bobby and Danny's "model deckhand" antics:''' Both Bobby and Danny are often seen bringing women back to the yacht after nights out, leading to confrontations with Hannah, the chief stew.
*'''Chef Ben's struggles:''' Ben faces challenges with the demanding guests and sometimes struggles to maintain a positive relationship with the stew team.
*'''Julia and Hannah's dynamic:''' The two stews sometimes clash over their approaches to guest service and their personal relationships with other crew members.
*'''Captain Mark's strict leadership:''' Captain Mark enforces strict rules and can be seen as a tough but fair leader on the yacht.
