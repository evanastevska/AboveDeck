The '''eighth season''' of [[Below Deck Mediterranean]] was aired on Bravo from September 25, 2023, for seventeen weeks and concluded on January 22, 2024. It aired a total of sixteen episodes.

== About ==
At the beginning of this season we saw the return of two crew members from the previous season, these were Captain [[Sandy Yawn]] and [[Natalya Scudder]] as a Temp Chief Stewardess and then a 3rd Stewardess once [[Below Deck Down Under]] [[Below Deck Down Under Season 1|Season 1]]'s 2nd Stewardess [[Tumi Mhlongo]] arrived as the permanent Chief Stewardess. [[Luka Brunton]] also joined this season, previously appearing in [[Below Deck Down Under Season 2]]. After the first charter we also saw the return of last years 2nd Steward [[Kyle Viljoen]].  

Captain Sandy was in charge of commanding the yacht ''Mustique'' in Italy, primarily in Genoa and the Italian Riviera during this charter season. ''Mustique'' is a 180-foot Trinity superyacht  boasting a spacious main salon with a baby grand piano, a formal dining room, a large sundeck with a jacuzzi, and a comprehensive selection of water toys including jet skis, paddleboards, and a seabob, all set against a retro interior design aesthetic with dark wood panelling and plush upholstery. It accommodates up to 12 guests in six plush cabins which are classically decorated.

== Notable Storylines ==

* '''Chief Stew drama:''' The main focus is the power struggle between Tumi and Natalya, with Kyle often playing a role in exacerbating their conflicts. 
* '''Kyle's manipulative behaviour:''' Kyle is portrayed as a major source of drama, often stirring up tension between other crew members and creating unnecessary conflict. 
* '''Crew member personal issues:''' Several crew members face personal challenges, including relationship issues and personal struggles that impact their work performance. 
* '''Staffing difficulties:''' The crew encounters challenges due to some members falling ill or needing to leave the boat unexpectedly, leading to a shortage of staff. 
* '''Boatmances:''' There are a few developing romantic relationships between crew members, which also contribute to the drama
