The '''seventh season''' of [[Below Deck Mediterranean]] was aired on Bravo from July 11, 2022, for nineteen weeks and concluded on November 22, 2022. It aired a total of twenty episodes.

== About ==
In this season we saw the return of three crew members from the previous season, these were Captain [[Sandy Yawn]] and Deckhand [[Mzi "Zee" Dempers]]. A third in to the season last years Stewardess [[Courtney Veale]] returned, but as one of this years deckhands. 

Captain Sandy was in charge of commanding the yacht ''Home'' around the the country island of Malta during this charter season. ''Home'' is a 163-foot motor superyacht with a sleek and modern design. It features a sundeck with a jacuzzi, sun lounges and a bar; an air-conditioned gym as well as water toys including seaBobs, hoverboards and jet skis. The yacht accommodates up to 12 guests in six cabins: a full-beam master suite can be found on the main deck, while a full-beam VIP cabin, two double cabins and two twins are found on the deck below.

== Notable Storylines ==

* '''Natasha and Dave's complicated romance:''' The main focus of the season is the tumultuous relationship between Natasha and Dave, with their personal drama often affecting the crew's dynamics and service quality. 
* '''Crew changes and leadership issues:''' The season features a new Bosun, Raygan Tyler, who faces challenges in establishing leadership on the deck team, leading to tension with other crew members. 
* '''Natalya and Storm's budding romance:''' Natalya and Storm develop a romantic connection throughout the season, navigating the challenges of a "boatmance". 
* '''Guest challenges:''' The crew encounters demanding guests with specific dietary needs and high expectations, putting pressure on both the interior and exterior teams. 
* '''Internal conflicts within the interior team:''' Aside from the Natasha and Dave drama, there are occasional conflicts within the interior team as they strive to deliver excellent service
