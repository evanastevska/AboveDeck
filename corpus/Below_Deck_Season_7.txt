The '''seventh season''' of ''[[Original Below Deck list|Below Deck]]'' was aired on Bravo from October 7, 2019, for twenty weeks and concluded on February 17, 2020.

== About ==
The cast at the beginning of this season saw the return of captain [[Lee Rosbach]], [[Kate Chastain]] and the newly promoted bosun [[Ashton Pienaar]] return. Last years deckhand [[Rhylee Gerber]] also returned halfway through the season after [[Abbi Murphy]] quit. The season was also the last to feature chief stewardess [[Kate Chastain]]. 

After a change of yacht for last year, this season saw the third and final appearance of the superyacht ''Valor''. It saw the crew navigate their way around the island of Phuket in southern Thailand.
