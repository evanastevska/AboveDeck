The '''eleventh season''' of ''[[Original Below Deck list|Below Deck]]'' was aired on Bravo from February 5, 2024, for seventeen weeks and concluded on May 27, 2024.

==About==
This was the very first season to not appear Captain [[Lee Rosbach]], he was replaced by [[Kerry Titheradge]] who featured in [[Below Deck Adventure]]. Chef [[Rachel Hargrove]] also didn't return after having previously appeared in seasons [[Below Deck Season 8|eight]], [[Below Deck Season 9|nine]] and [[Below Deck Season 10|ten]]. However [[Fraser Olender]] and [[Ben Willoughby]] did return as the Chief Steward and Lead Deckhand respectively. Throughout the whole season it featured 13 crew members; an equal record set by the [[Below Deck Season 10|previous season]]. 

For the second season the superyacht ''St.David'' was used for the show, which is a 60-meter motor yacht with a sky lounge, spiral staircase, sun deck with sunlounge's and a spa pool. It has sleeping facilities for up to 12 guests including a split-level master suite. The destination for this charter season was filmed around the island of Grenada in the Caribbean Sea.

== Notable Storylines ==

* '''New Captain:''' Captain Kerry Titheradge takes over command. 
* '''Crew Dynamics:''' New crew members alongside some returning faces with new roles. 
* '''Interior Drama:''' Focus on conflicts within the interior team, particularly with "singer" Barbie. 
* '''Challenges on Deck:''' New deckhands face difficulties adjusting to their roles. 
* '''Romance on Board:''' Several crew members develop romantic relationships. 
* '''Guest Issues:''' Dealing with demanding and sometimes "rude" charter guests.
