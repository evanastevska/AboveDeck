The '''ninth season''' of [[Below Deck Mediterranean]] was aired on Bravo from June 3, 2024, for sixteen weeks and concluded on September 23, 2024. It aired a total of seventeen episodes.

== About ==
This season saw the return of Captain [[Sandy Yawn]] for the eighth season consecutively, and [[Below Deck Mediterranean Season 7|season sevens]] Stewardess [[Elena Dubaich]]. Also returning was [[Below Deck Mediterranean Season 4|seasons four]] and [[Below Deck Mediterranean Season 5|five's]] 2nd Stewardess [[Aesha Scott]] as this years Chief Stewardess. 

Captain Sandy was in charge of commanding the yacht ''Mustique'' around Athens and the Greek Islands during this charter season. For the second season running the superyacht ''Mustique'' was used. It is a 54.9-meter Trinity motor yacht which featured a skylounge with a marble-topped bar, baby grand piano and many water toys including waterski's, paddleboards and wave runners. The yacht accommodates up to 12 guests in six luxury cabins.

== Notable Storylines ==

* '''Love Triangle Drama:''' Joe's flirtatious behaviour with both Bri and Ellie creates major conflict between the two women, with accusations of mixed signals and manipulation coming from both sides.
* '''Aesha as Chief Stew:''' Aesha's leadership style is put to the test as she tries to manage the drama between the interior crew while ensuring the guests' needs are met. 
* '''Chef Challenges:''' Chef Jono faces difficulties with guest dietary restrictions and creating meals under pressure, especially during the storm. 
* '''Storm at Sea:''' A significant storm hits the yacht, causing major issues for the deck crew and forcing the interior crew to adapt to challenging conditions. 
* '''Guest Issues:''' The season features a variety of demanding guests, including some who create complications with their special requests and behaviour.
* '''Captain Sandy's Leadership:''' Captain Sandy is often called upon to mediate conflicts between the crew members and make tough decisions in difficult situations
