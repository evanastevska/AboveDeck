[[Original Below Deck list|'''Below Deck''']] first aired on Bravo on July 1, 2013. It aired for 11 weeks and concluded on September 9, 2013. 

== About ==
Below Deck featured nine cast members with various yachting experiences, who embark on a five week charter season. It is filmed in St. Maarten, which is in the Lesser Antilles in the Caribbean onboard a 50-meter (164-foot) yacht called ''Cuor di Leone,'' which had a sundeck, jacuzzi, outdoor cinema, water toys and accommodates up to 12 guests in six en-suite cabins.  The yacht was renamed ''Honor'' for the series, and is captained by [[Lee Rosbach]].

== Notable Storylines ==
*'''New Faces:'''  This season introduces new crew members like Kat Held, a strong-willed chief stew, and Adrienne Gang, a charismatic deckhand, who bring new dynamics to the group.
*'''Chef Ben's Struggles:'''  Chef Ben faces pressure in the galley due to difficult guest requests and occasional conflicts with the interior crew regarding food service.
*'''Samantha "Sam" Tarrant's Storyline:'''  Sam, a returning stew, deals with personal issues and drama with other crew members, particularly regarding her relationship with a guest.
*'''Captain Lee's Leadership:'''  Captain Lee is seen navigating conflicts between crew members, maintaining order on the boat, and ensuring the smooth running of charters.
*'''Guest Drama:'''  The season features a variety of demanding guests, including some who create tension with their high expectations and difficult behaviour.
