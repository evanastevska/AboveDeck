The '''eighth season''' of ''[[Original Below Deck list|Below Deck]]'' was aired on Bravo from November 2, 2020, for seventeen weeks and concluded on February 22, 2021.

== About ==
In this season Captain [[Lee Rosbach]] was the only returning cast member from the previous year. However we did see the return of Bosun [[Eddie Lucas]], who last appeared in [[Below Deck Season 3|season three]]. This meant there were six new crew members, including [[Rachel Hargrove]] as the yachts chef. The vessel used for the eighth season saw the return of [[Below Deck Season 6|season six's]] ''My Seanna'', which is a 184.97 ft superyacht that accommodates up to twelve guests. During this season it was navigated through the waters around the island of Antigua.

== Notable Storylines ==

* '''Elizabeth and James's Fling:''' The tumultuous relationship between Elizabeth Frankini and James Hough
* '''Chef Rachel's Conflicts:''' Rachel faces challenges with the interior team regarding food service, and also deals with personal struggles that affect her work. 
* '''Shane's Issues on Deck:''' New deckhand Shane is frequently criticized for his work ethic and attitude, leading to friction with Eddie Lucas. 
* '''Izzy and Rob's Relationship Drama:''' Izzy's relationship with Rob on the interior team becomes strained, causing tension within the crew 
* '''COVID-19 Impact:''' The season unexpectedly ends early due to the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic, leaving the crew to grapple with the uncertainty of their jobs.
