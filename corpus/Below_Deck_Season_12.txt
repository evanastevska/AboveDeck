'''Season 12''' of ''[[Below Deck]]'' premiered on June 2, 2025.

==Background==
The season premiered following the conclusion of the [[Below Deck Down Under Season 3|third season]] of ''[[Below Deck Down Under]]''. The season filmed on St. David for its third season in a row. Four cast members from the previous season returned including: Captain Kerry Titheradge, Chief Steward Fraser Oleander, Deckhand Kyle Stillie, and Chef Anthony Iracane. Iracane was fired last season due to poor job performance, but was asked back when Chef Lawrence Snowden quit on the first charter due to not being able to handle the pressures of the charter and being on reality television.

=== Notable Storylines ===

* Crew Dynamics: There are several new crew members alongside returning faces.
* Bosun Makes Mistakes : Bosun Caio struggles with leadership and his responsibilities leading to increasing concern from Captain Kerry as he makes several mistakes.  As things fail to improve Caio is soon replaced with someone more suitable for a leadership role.
* Boatmance: A stew and a deckhand have a growing attraction to each other but for one of them it soon seems to fizzle out.
* Guest Issues: Rude and demanding guests lead to stress for the stews and tension on board.
