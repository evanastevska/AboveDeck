The '''tenth season''' of ''[[Original Below Deck list|Below Deck]]'' was aired on Bravo from November 21, 2022, for seventeen weeks and concluded on March 20, 2023.

== About ==
Captain [[Lee Rosbach]] returned as captain for his tenth season, however due to mobility issues as a result of back surgery, he had to step away a third into the season. He was replaced by ''[[Below Deck Mediterranean]]'' captain, [[Sandy Yawn]], who captained the ship for ten episodes. Rosbach returned the final four episodes of the season. [[Rachel Hargrove]] also returned, this being her final season on the show, along with [[Fraser Olender]] as the new Chief Steward (a first for the franchise). Throughout the whole season we saw 13 crew members, most of any season so far. 

Filming during this season took place on a new boat called ''St, David''; a 60-meter motor yacht which featured a sky lounge, spiral staircase, sun deck with sun lounges and a spa pool. It has six cabins, including a split-level master suite, accommodating up to 12 guests. The destination for this charter season was filmed in the Caribbean Sea around the island of St. Lucia.

== Notable Storylines ==

* '''Captain Lee's health issues:''' A major plot point is Captain Lee having to step away from the yacht due to a medical condition, leading to a temporary change in leadership. 
* '''New crew dynamics:''' The season introduces new crew members like Camille Lamb (deck/stew) and Ben Willoughby (deckhand), whose relationship becomes a central focus. 
* '''Camille's controversial behaviour:''' Camille's work ethic and attitude towards her job are often criticized by other crew members, creating drama on board. 
* '''Chef Rachel's challenges:''' Chef Rachel faces pressure to deliver high-quality meals amidst demanding charter guests. 
* '''Internal conflicts:''' The interior crew, particularly Alissa and Camille, experience tension and disagreements.
