The '''fourth''' '''season''' of [[Below Deck Mediterranean]] was aired on Bravo from June 3, 2019, for seventeen weeks and concluded on September 30, 2017. It aired a total of eighteen episodes.

==About==
At the beginning of this season we saw four crew members return from the previous season; this included Captain [[Sandy Yawn]],  Chief Stewardess [[Hannah Ferrier]], deckhand [[Colin Macy-O'Toole]] and newly promoted Bosun [[João Franco]]. In the final third of the season we saw [[Below Deck Mediterranean Season 1|season one's]] [[Ben Robinson]] as the yachts chef.

Captain Sandy was in charge of commanding the yacht ''Sirocco'' around the French coast during the charter season. ''Sirocco'' previously appeared in [[Below Deck Mediterranean Season 2|season two]], and is a 47-metre long superyacht. It features include a jacuzzi, gym, a movie theatre and various water toys. The yacht accommodates up to 12 guests, including a master suite, a VIP cabin, two double cabins, and two twin cabins.

==Notable Storylines ==

*'''Chef Drama:''' Mila faces criticism for her cooking abilities, leading to her departure, Anastasia taking over as chef despite lacking full culinary training and Ben cooking for the last two charters in a broken galley
*'''Jack and Aesha's Relationship:''' A romance begins with Aesha and Jack, leading to him asking her a question at the end of the season
*'''Travis Alcohol Struggles:''' Travis struggles to hide and accept his issues with alcohol, with all crew having strong opinions on it, including Captain Sandy
*'''Joao's Personal Struggles:''' Joao, the Bosun, deals with the aftermath of a past relationship and attempts to regain respect from the crew.
*'''Stewardess Dynamics:''' Hannah faces challenges managing the interior team, particularly with the addition of a new, less experienced third stew, June.
*'''Deckhand Conflicts:''' Tension arises between the two bromances within the deck crew (Joao & Colin) and (Travis & Jack)
*'''Captain Sandy's Leadership:''' Captain Sandy is often called upon to mediate crew conflicts and address issues related to guest satisfaction.
