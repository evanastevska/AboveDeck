The '''fifth season''' of ''[[Original Below Deck list|Below Deck]]'' was aired on Bravo from September 5, 2017, for fifteen weeks and concluded on December 12, 2017.

== About ==
For this season most of the cast were new and extremely green, with little yachting experience. Captain [[Lee Rosbach]], chief stewardess [[Kate Chastain]] and [[Nico Scholly]] were the only returning crew. Last years deckhand [[Kyle Dixon]] joined the crew in episode eleven after bosun [[EJ Jansen]] left to return to a boat he had previously been working on. Also returning this season was the 154-foot superyacht ''Valor'', however this year captain Lee would be navigating it around the Caribbean.
