The '''third season''' of ''[[Original Below Deck list|Below Deck]]'' was aired on Bravo from August 25, 2015, for fifteen weeks and concluded on December 1, 2015.

== About ==
This seasons cast saw the return of [[Lee Rosbach]] as captain, [[Kate Chastain]] as chief stewardess, [[Eddie Lucas]] as bosun, and [[Amy Johnson]] as this years 2nd stewardess. As a result of captain Lee firing a few crew members [[David Bradberry]] returned as a deckhand (ep 11 - 13) and [[Ben Robinson]] as the yachts chef (ep 10 - 13).

The yacht for this season was a 161-foot Trinity Yachts vessel called ''Stay Salty'' which has a spacious tri-deck with a saloon, sky lounge, and jacuzzi. For the purpose of the show they named it ''Eros'' and filmed in and around The Bahamas.

== Notable Storylines ==

* '''Emile/Rocky/Eddie Triangle:''' Throughout the season Emile tried to seduce Rocky, who was secretly hooking up with Eddie, who actually had a long term girlfriend back home 
* '''Galley Fire:''' A fire in the galley resulted in new chef Leon getting fired, caused a divide with the crew
* '''Interior Staff Dynamic:''' Kate and Amy resolved their issues from last season becoming friends, and Rocky's attitude caused friction amongst the stewardesses
* '''Many Deckhands:''' From Don quitting a third into the season due to feeling overqualified, and Dane being fired due to his behaviour to the return of David Bradberry
