The '''ninth season''' of ''[[Original Below Deck list|Below Deck]]'' was aired on Bravo from October 25, 2021, for fifteen weeks and concluded on February 7, 2022.

== About ==
The start of the ninth season saw a different captain for the first time, in the shape of [[Sean Meagher]]; however this was only temporary while captain [[Lee Rosbach]] had some health issues. Also returning this year were [[Eddie Lucas]] who was promoted to First Officer at the end of the previous season and Chef [[Rachel Hargrove]]. This season was the first for future Chief Steward [[Fraser Olender]]. It also saw the third and final appearance of the 184.97 ft superyacht ''My Seanna''. This season saw it sail through the Caribbean Sea around the waters of St. Kitts.

== Notable Storylines ==

* '''Eddie's Relationship Issues:''' significant focus is on Eddie's personal life as he navigates the aftermath of a relationship breakdown 
* '''Jake and Rayna's Dynamic:''' The dynamic between Jake and Rayna is explored, with them trying to understand their feelings for each other after a significant revelation from Jake.
* '''Fraser's Insecurity:''' Fraser experiences a drop in confidence when he loses his walkie-talkie, impacting his sense of competence on the job.
* '''Heather's Leadership Struggles:''' Heather is determined to improve the interior team's performance, which leads to tension with her stews.
* '''Challenging Charter Guests:''' The crew faces a particularly difficult group of charter guests who are known for their wild behaviour
