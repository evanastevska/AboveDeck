The '''sixth''' season of [[Below Deck Mediterranean]] was aired on Bravo from June 28, 2021, for seventeen weeks and concluded on October 18, 2021. It aired a total of eighteen episodes.

==About==
From the previous season the only returning cast member was Captain [[Sandy Yawn]]; however we did see the return of [[Below Deck Mediterranean Season 2|season two's]] deckhand [[Malia White]] as this seasons Bosun. 

Captain Sandy was in charge of commanding the yacht ''Lady Michelle'' around Croatia, with the home port of Šibenik during this charter season. ''Lady Michelle'' is a 55 m (180 ft 5 in) super-yacht with traditional styling, glossy wood finishes, and a nautical blue and white colour scheme. It features an open-air cinema, a spacious aft deck, a sundeck with a spa pool, gymnasium, drinks bar, sun loungers, and al fresco dining area. The yacht accommodates up to 12 guests in six staterooms.

== Notable Storylines ==

* '''New Leadership:''' Katie takes on the role of Chief Stew, bringing a new leadership style to the team, while Captain Sandy deals with integrating a fresh crew. 
* '''Chef Mathew's Challenges:''' Chef Mathew, known for his experience with high-profile clients, faces pressure to deliver exceptional cuisine while dealing with unexpected challenges and personal setbacks. 
* '''Deck Crew Dynamics:''' The deck team struggles with a new, inexperienced deckhand, causing friction and challenges during demanding maneuvers. 
* '''Interpersonal Conflicts:''' Significant tension arises between crew members, including conflicts between the interior and exterior teams, and personal drama amongst the stews. 
* '''Guest Drama:''' The season features a range of challenging charter guests with demanding requests, creating additional pressure for the crew
