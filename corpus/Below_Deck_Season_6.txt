The '''sixth season''' of ''[[Original Below Deck list|Below Deck]]'' was aired on Bravo from October 2, 2018, for seventeen weeks and concluded on February 5, 2019.

== About ==
For this season only captain [[Lee Rosbach]] and chief stewardess [[Kate Chastain]] returned. This season featured the first male stew in the form of [[Josiah Carter]]. It also featured [[Caroline Bedol]] and [[Chandler Brooks]] who became the first stewardess to quit her job and the first bosun to be fired in [[Original Below Deck list|Below Deck]] history.

The yacht used for this season was the luxury superyacht ''Starship'', which was known as ''My Seanna'' for the purpose of the show. Its features included a spacious beach club, onboard cinema and jacuzzi. At 56 metres, ''My Seanna'' is the largest motor yacht to ever appear on [[Below Deck (Franchise)|Below Deck]]. Season six was filmed in February/March 2018 around the tropical waters of Tahiti.

==Notable Storylines==

*'''Chef drama:''' A charter guest shows romantic interest towards Chef Adrian, causing potential complications.
*'''Caroline's Struggles:''' Caroline's negativity and poor work ethic creates tension in the interior team, leading to clashes with Kate and other crew members
*'''Chandler's Leadership Issues:''' Chandler's decisions as Bosun often led to problems on the deck, causing frustration among the crew and drawing criticism from Captain Lee.
*'''Crew Dynamics:''' Romantic entanglements and personal conflicts between crew members, particularly between Ashton and Rhylee, add to the drama.
*'''Captain Lee's Leadership:''' Captain Lee is forced to address issues within the crew, including confronting Chandler about his performance and making changes to the deck team
