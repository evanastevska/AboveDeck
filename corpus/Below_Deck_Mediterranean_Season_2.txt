The '''second season''' of [[Below Deck Mediterranean]] was aired on Bravo from May 2, 2017, for fifteen weeks and concluded on August 15, 2017. It aired a total of fifteen episodes.

==About==
This seasons cast saw the return of Chief Stewardess [[Hannah Ferrier]] and deckhand [[Bobby Giancola]]. It was also the first season for Captain [[Sandy Yawn]], the first female captain in the franchise. 

Captain Sandy was in charge of commanding the yacht ''Sirocco'' through the Croatian Adriatic Sea during this charter season. ''Sirocco'' was a 47-metre long superyacht with a refined style of hardwood furniture and ebony accents. It features include a jacuzzi, gym, a movie theatre and various water toys. The yacht accommodates up to 12 guests, including a master suite, a VIP cabin, two double cabins, and two twin cabins.

== Notable Storylines ==

* '''Bobby and Hannah's conflict:''' Bobby's attempts to pursue romance with various crew members, including Malia, causes friction with Hannah, particularly when he accuses Lauren of an affair with a friend, leading to a major rift within the crew.
*'''"Naked" charter guests:''' The crew faces challenges maintaining professionalism while dealing with guests who are very open with their nudity.
*'''Bugsy's personal struggle:''' Bugsy deals with the grief of losing her grandmother and must decide whether to continue working on the yacht.
*'''Malia's love triangle:''' Malia finds herself caught between Chef Adam and Bosun Wes, creating a romantic storyline.
*'''Captain Sandy's leadership:''' Captain Sandy navigates the crew's interpersonal conflicts and ensures smooth operations on the yacht.
